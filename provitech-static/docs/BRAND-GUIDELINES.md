# BRAND GUIDELINES — PROVITECH INTEGRATORS

This document codifies the visual identity standards for **PROVITECH INTEGRATORS**, establishing strict rules for logo representation, color roles, typography, and visual assets.

---

## 1. Brand Identity Overview

- **Brand Name:** PROVITECH INTEGRATORS
- **Industry Sector:** Physical & Network Security System Integrations
- **Core Values:** Reliability, Clarity, Technical Precision, Peace of Mind
- **Visual Personality:** Contemporary, Dependable, Engineered, Confident

---

## 2. Official Logo System

### A. Master Brand Mark
The master logo combines three distinct elements:
1. The **PROVITECH** wordmark with an embedded dome surveillance camera in the "O" and an electric blue accent chevron in the "V".
2. The **INTEGRATORS** tracking wordmark enclosed in bilateral balance lines.
3. The geometric 3:1 proportional layout.

### B. Asset Specifications
- **Master File:** `assets/images/brand/provitech-logo.png`
- **Native Dimensions:** 2172 × 724 px
- **Aspect Ratio:** Exact 3:1
- **File Types:** PNG (transparent alpha channel), WebP (web optimized), SVG (future vector master)

### C. Clear Space Rules
- Minimum clear space on all four sides must be equal to or greater than the height of the capital letter **"I"** in `INTEGRATORS`.
- No typography, borders, background seams, or competing graphics may encroach into this protective perimeter.

### D. Permitted Backgrounds
1. **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary recommended application.
2. **Light Grey (`#F1F5F9`):** Approved for subtle content cards and alternating sections.
3. **Dark Charcoal / Deep Navy (`#25282D` / `#061B49`):** Permitted **only** when the logo is housed inside a clean, solid white protective plate with internal padding (as demonstrated in the official footer).

### E. Prohibited Logo Alterations ("Never Do This")
- Do **NOT** stretch, squeeze, condense, or distort the 3:1 aspect ratio.
- Do **NOT** recolor the logo elements (e.g., turning the electric blue chevron green or red).
- Do **NOT** add drop shadows, outer glows, strokes, or gradient overlays to the artwork.
- Do **NOT** rotate or angle the mark.
- Do **NOT** crop out the camera symbol or the word `INTEGRATORS`.
- Do **NOT** place directly over busy, high-contrast photographic backgrounds without a protective overlay.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy (Montserrat)

Montserrat is the exclusive brand typeface across digital and print touchpoints.

| Hierarchy Level | Font Size | Weight | Line Height | Usage |

| **Display / Hero H1** | `clamp(2.375rem, 5.5vw, 4rem)` (38px–64px) | **800** ExtraBold | `1.1` | Main hero statement |
| **Section Title H2** | `clamp(1.875rem, 3.5vw, 2.5rem)` (30px–40px) | **700** Bold | `1.2` | Major section headings |
| **Subheading H3** | `1.25rem`–`1.5rem` (20px–24px) | **600** SemiBold | `1.3` | Service card titles, product titles |
| **Card Heading H4** | `1.125rem` (18px) | **600** SemiBold | `1.4` | Form headings, approach step titles |
| **Eyebrow / Kicker** | `0.75rem` (12px) | **700** Bold | `1.2` | Section identifiers, uppercase (`letter-spacing: 0.2em`) |

| **Legal / Metadata** | `0.75rem` (12px) | **400** Regular | `1.5` | Footer copyright, disclaimer text |
